
# 🌍 ESG Investment Analytics Framework (SQL)

## 📌 Problem Statement

Investors are increasingly integrating Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) factors into decision-making. However, ESG data and financial performance data often exist in silos, making it difficult to evaluate companies holistically.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To build a structured analytics framework that combines ESG metrics with financial data to support investment decisions such as **Buy, Hold, or Engage**.

---

## 🛠️ Approach

### Data Integration

* Combined ESG datasets (carbon emissions, governance scores, social indicators)
* Integrated financial metrics (revenue, profitability, volatility)
* Standardized datasets for comparability across 20 global companies

### Data Processing (SQL)

* Cleaned and validated raw datasets
* Built joins across ESG and financial tables
* Created derived metrics (ESG composite score, risk indicators)

### Analytical Framework

* Designed scoring logic for ESG performance
* Segmented companies based on:

  * ESG strength
  * Financial stability
* Mapped outputs into:

  * **Buy** → Strong ESG + Strong financials
  * **Hold** → Moderate ESG / Stable financials
  * **Engage** → Weak ESG but improvable

---

## 📊 Key Insights

* Companies with stronger ESG scores demonstrated **more stable financial performance**
* High carbon emitters frequently showed **governance and risk management gaps**
* Several firms identified as **“Engage” candidates**, where active intervention could improve ESG outcomes

---

## 💼 Business Impact

This framework can support:

* ESG-integrated portfolio construction
* Risk-adjusted investment strategies
* Engagement-based investing decisions
